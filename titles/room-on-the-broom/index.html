How to check availability in your country

  1. Find the title on ReelOpal and open its title page.
  2. Use the red official-provider button — it opens the title's official page (Apple TV store for movies, TVMaze for series).
  3. Make sure you are signed in to the store or service for your country (USA, UK or your EU country) — catalogues are licensed per country.
  4. For a series, note the network shown on the title page (for example Netflix, HBO, BBC One): that network's own service or site is the legal home in most regions.
  5. If a title is not on your usual service, check the other official providers in the table below before assuming it is unavailable.

Where to Watch — Questions

Is ReelOpal legal?

Yes. ReelOpal is a discovery guide: it publishes metadata, ratings, cast and trailers from legal sources (the TVMaze API and Apple's official iTunes charts) and links out to official provider pages. It hosts no streams, no downloads and no pirated content.

How do I check if a title is available in my country?

Open the title page, follow the official provider button, and check while signed in to your own region. Streaming catalogues differ between the USA, UK and EU, and the provider page always shows the options for your country.

Why does availability differ between the USA and the UK?

Studios license titles country by country, so the same film or series can sit on different services — or none — in the US, UK and EU. That is why ReelOpal labels every chart title with its region and never promises one worldwide provider.

Does ReelOpal host streams or downloads?

No. ReelOpal hosts no streams and no downloads. Every title links to its official provider page, where you can watch it legally.

About ReelOpal — what this site is (and is not)

ReelOpal is a legal movies & series discovery guide for viewers in the United States and Europe. It publishes 176 real titles with ratings, genres, cast, seasons, episode counts and official trailers, and links every title to its official provider page.

ReelOpal is not a streaming site. It hosts no streams, no downloads and no pirated content, and it never will. When you press “Watch”, you leave ReelOpal for the title's official provider.

Data sources: series metadata, posters, cast and episode counts come from the TVMaze API; movie charts and artwork come from Apple's official iTunes charts for the US and UK stores; trailers are official YouTube uploads. Catalog snapshot: 16 Nov 2026.

Canonical address: https://reelopal.onrender.com — this Render URL is the site's canonical address until it moves to its own custom domain, when the canonical, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt must all be re-pointed to that domain.

How we rank: Netflix Top 10 & charts

Series data, ratings, cast and episode counts come from the TVMaze API; the Netflix ranking orders Netflix-carried series by TVMaze popularity weight, cross-checked with audience ratings. Movie charts are Apple's official iTunes store charts — the US store for the American chart and the UK store for Europe. Nothing is scraped from pirate sites and no streams or downloads are hosted.

US vs. Europe: why two charts?

Viewing habits differ across the Atlantic. ReelOpal publishes both the US chart and the UK/European chart side by side and labels every movie with its region, so a reader in Berlin or New York sees the ranking that matches their stores and services.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the #1 trending series on Netflix right now?

Black Mirror leads the ReelOpal Netflix Top 10 — a popularity ranking of Netflix series built from TVMaze data (snapshot: Nov 16, 2026), not Netflix's official daily chart. See the full ranking on the Netflix Top 10 page.

What changed in the Netflix Top 10 this week?

This is the first snapshot (16 Nov 2026), so there is no previous week to compare with yet. The ranking is a frozen snapshot, rebuilt with each catalog update — snapshot: 16 Nov 2026. It is ReelOpal's ranking, not Netflix's official daily chart.

Does ReelOpal host movies or episodes?

No. ReelOpal is a discovery guide. It publishes legal metadata, ratings, cast and trailers, and every title links out to its official provider page, where you can watch it legally.

Where does the data come from?

Series data, posters, cast and episode counts come from the TVMaze API. Movie charts and artwork come from Apple's official iTunes charts for the US and UK stores. Series snapshot: November 15, 2026; movie charts: November 14, 2026.

Do I need an account to use ReelOpal?

No. Browsing, search, filters, the watchlist, the full episode breakdown, the HD poster pack and the watchlist export need no account — everything is open.

Can I use ReelOpal in Europe?

Yes. ReelOpal tracks both the US and UK/European movie charts and marks each title with its region.

How do I choose what to watch tonight?

Use the mood picker on the home page, or start from the Netflix Top 10 snapshot and the US and UK movie charts. Every title page shows the rating, genres, cast and a link to the official provider, so you can decide in under a minute and watch legally.

Is a title on Netflix USA?

Title pages show the network or provider recorded in the catalog data. Netflix catalogues differ by country, so open the title's official provider page and check while signed in to your own region — that page always shows the options for your country. See the Where to Watch guide.

Is ReelOpal legal?

Yes. ReelOpal is a discovery guide: it publishes metadata, ratings, cast and trailers from legal sources (the TVMaze API and Apple's official iTunes charts) and links out to official provider pages. It hosts no streams, no downloads and no pirated content.

How does the Netflix Top 10 snapshot work?

ReelOpal ranks Netflix-carried series by TVMaze popularity weight, cross-checked against audience ratings. The current snapshot is dated 16 Nov 2026. It is a popularity snapshot — not Netflix's official daily chart, which Netflix publishes itself and which changes daily by country.

Where can I watch a title legally?

Open the title page and use the official provider button. Movies link to their official Apple TV store page (US or UK store); series link to their official TVMaze page, which lists the network and official links. The Where to Watch guide explains how to check availability in your country.

How does the watchlist work?

Tap the plus button on any poster, or “Add to watchlist” on a title page. The list is stored in this browser only (localStorage), so it is private to this device and needs no account. You can export it as a file from the Watchlist page.

What are the best movies on Netflix in November 2026?

From ReelOpal's own catalog data: the Netflix Top 10 snapshot page ranks the most popular Netflix series (TVMaze-popularity snapshot, 16 Nov 2026), and the Best of 2026 page lists every 2026 title on the US and UK charts. For the UK, see the Best for UK Viewers page. Neither ranking is Netflix's official chart.

What is the “Sponsored · Smartlink” pick?

It is one sponsored link shown across the site. When you tap it, our ad partner matches you to a trending movie, series or streaming offer for your country (US & Europe) and opens it in a new tab. It is advertising — it never replaces a title's official provider link, and ReelOpal still hosts no streams or downloads.

Analytics — visitor dashboard, GA4 & Plausible setup

Your visitor dashboard is live at /dashboard/ (also linked in the main menu and footer). It works with zero backend: it reads only what window.ReelOpalAnalytics records in this browser's localStorage — page views (all time + today), a per-day unique-visitor flag, top titles, top pages, referrer type (search / social / direct / other, from document.referrer plus UTM parameters), smartlink clicks. It is privacy-friendly: no cookies, so no cookie banner is needed. Until GA4 or Plausible is connected, the dashboard shows this browser's own visits only — a per-device estimate, not site-wide traffic.

Connect GA4 (Google Analytics 4) — for real site-wide traffic:

  1. Go to analytics.google.com and sign in with the site's Google account.
  2. Click Admin → Create → Property, name it “ReelOpal”, set your time zone and currency, then choose Web as the platform and enter the site's URL.
  3. Google shows a Measurement ID that looks like G-XXXXXXXXXX — copy it.
  4. Open the site code, find GA_MEASUREMENT_ID at the top (next to SMARTLINK_URL), paste the ID between the quotes, save and redeploy. The dashboard's GA4 status then flips to Connected.

Connect Plausible (privacy-friendly alternative):

  1. Go to plausible.io, create an account and choose Add a website.
  2. Enter the site's domain exactly as Plausible asks (for example reelopal.onrender.com or your custom domain).
  3. Open the site code, find PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN at the top, paste that same domain between the quotes, save and redeploy. The dashboard's Plausible status then flips to Connected.

Plain note: real site-wide traffic — every visitor, not just this browser — only appears once GA4 or Plausible is connected. Until then, the dashboard honestly shows this browser's own visits only, and shows 0 with “No visits recorded yet on this browser” when nothing has been recorded. Also at deploy: verify the domain in Google Search Console, submit sitemap.xml, and the canonical, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt already point at the Render domain — re-point them only if a custom domain is set. You can inspect the local hook anytime with ReelOpalAnalytics.summary() in the browser console.
