Where to Watch Legally in the USA

Direct answer: Every title on ReelOpal links to its official provider only — movies to their official Apple TV store page (US or UK store), series to their official TVMaze page with the network and official links. ReelOpal hosts no streams, no downloads and no pirated links. Availability differs by country, so always confirm on the provider page for your region.

Updated: 16 Nov 2026 · Provider data: catalog snapshot 16 Nov 2026 (TVMaze networks + Apple TV charts).

How to check availability in your country

  1. Find the title on ReelOpal and open its title page.
  2. Use the red official-provider button — it opens the title's official page (Apple TV store for movies, TVMaze for series).
  3. Make sure you are signed in to the store or service for your country (USA, UK or your EU country) — catalogues are licensed per country.
  4. For a series, note the network shown on the title page (for example Netflix, HBO, BBC One): that network's own service or site is the legal home in most regions.
  5. If a title is not on your usual service, check the other official providers in the table below before assuming it is unavailable.

USA, UK & EU — what differs

USA: US-chart movies link to the Apple TV US store (prices in USD). Series pages show the US network recorded by TVMaze — Netflix, HBO (Max), CBS, NBC, ABC, FOX, AMC and others. See also Best for US Viewers.

UK: UK-chart movies link to the Apple TV UK store (prices in GBP). UK networks in the data include BBC One, ITV1 and Channel 4. See also Best for UK Viewers and the best movies on Netflix UK November 2026 picks there.

EU: There is no single EU catalogue — each country is licensed separately. Use the same check: open the official provider page signed in to your own country's store or service. The UK chart is ReelOpal's closest European signal in the current data.

Watching from the EU

There is no single EU catalogue: each EU country is licensed separately, and ReelOpal's catalog data has no separate EU chart — the UK Apple TV chart is the closest European signal in the current data. To check a title, open its official provider page signed in to your own country's store or service; the provider table above lists the networks recorded in the catalog.

Where to Watch — Questions

Is ReelOpal legal?

Yes. ReelOpal is a discovery guide: it publishes metadata, cast and official watch links from legal sources (the TVMaze API and Apple's official iTunes charts) and links out to official provider pages. It hosts no streams, no downloads and no pirated content.

How do I check if a title is available in my country?

Open the title page, follow the official provider button, and check while signed in to your own region. Streaming catalogues differ between the USA, UK and EU, and the provider page always shows the options for your country.

Why does availability differ between the USA and the UK?

Studios license titles country by country, so the same film or series can sit on different services — or none — in the US, UK and EU. That is why ReelOpal labels every chart title with its region and never promises one worldwide provider.

Does ReelOpal host streams or downloads?

No. ReelOpal hosts no streams and no downloads. Every title links to its official provider page, where you can watch it legally.
